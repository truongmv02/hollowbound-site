Privacy Policy

Developer: Gamelor · Package: com.gamelor.hollowbound · Effective date: November 3, 2026

In short: Hollowbound does not collect, transmit or share any personal or device data. It needs no account, shows no ads, has no in-app purchases and works fully offline. Your progress is kept only on your device.

This policy applies to the mobile game Hollowbound (the "Game") published by Gamelor ("we", "us", "our") on Google Play and the Apple App Store. Jump to: Data Safety · Data Retention and Deletion · Children · Contact.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask you to create an account or to sign in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files or any other personal data.

We do not collect device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID, IMEI or IP address, and we do not track your activity in the Game or across other apps and websites.

2. Third-party services

The Game does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, social login or in-app purchase SDKs. No data is sent from the Game to us or to any third party.

3. Device permissions

The Game does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone, SMS, calendar or shared storage.

4. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (unlocked heroes, items, in-game currencies, settings and similar game state) in the Game's private storage on your device. This data does not leave your device through the Game.

If you have turned on system backups, Android or iOS may back up app data to your Google or Apple account. Those backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, not by us.

5. Information from app stores

When you download the Game, Google Play or the Apple App Store may collect information about you under their own terms and privacy policies. They may show us aggregated statistics, such as install counts, ratings and crash rates, that do not identify you. We do not receive personal data from them.

6. When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use it only to answer your request. We do not add you to mailing lists and do not share your message with anyone.

7. How we use and share information

Because we do not collect personal information through the Game, we do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we do not sell, rent, trade or share it with anyone.

8. Security

Game data stays in the Game's private storage on your device and is protected by your device's own security. The Game does not transmit any data over the network. Support emails are kept in a password-protected mailbox.

9. Data Safety

This summary matches the answers in the Data safety section of our Google Play listing.

  • Data collected: none. No personal, device or usage data is collected.
  • Data shared with third parties: none.
  • Encryption in transit: not applicable, the Game does not transmit data.
  • Data deletion: available to every user, see section 10.
  • Ads: none. Account required: no. In-app purchases: none.

10. Data retention and deletion

Game progress is kept on your device for as long as the Game is installed. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request, and at most 12 months after the last message.

All game data is stored only on your device, so you can delete it yourself at any time:

  • Android: Settings > Apps > Hollowbound > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the Game.
  • iOS: delete the Game from your device. To also remove it from iCloud, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

The Game has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. To delete any support emails you have sent us, email us from the same address with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete them and confirm within 30 days.

11. Children and families

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or the minimum age of digital consent in their country. The Game contains no ads, no purchases, no chat, no social features and no links that lead players to collect or share personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA/CPRA in California, or Vietnam's Decree 13/2023/ND-CP), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we hold no personal data from the Game, there is normally nothing to access or delete. For support emails you have sent us, contact us and we will act on your request within 30 days.

13. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game adds features that handle personal data, such as advertising, analytics, accounts or purchases, we will update this policy and our Google Play Data safety declaration before that version is released, and change the effective date above.
