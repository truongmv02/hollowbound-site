Hollowbound

A pixel-art roguelite where every reload is a gamble.

Hold to fire and your hero aims for you. Your job is to dodge the swarm, manage your magazine, and survive long enough to face the boss at the end of the map.

What you'll find

Short runs, a deep arsenal and a world that gets darker the further you go.

Hold to fire, auto aim

No aiming stick. Your hero targets the nearest enemy, so you focus on movement.

Reload is the risk

Every weapon runs on a magazine. The reload window is when the swarm closes in.

Eight heroes

From the Guardian to the Summoner, each class plays and builds differently.

Five maps, seventy-five stages

Randomised rooms, elite leaders mid-map and a map boss waiting at stage 15.

A large arsenal

Guns, blades and staves that combine fire patterns, elements and weapon skills.

Plays offline

No account and no connection needed. Your progress is saved on your device.
